# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers align technical execution with delivery goals, team capacity, and engineering quality. They help translate product priorities into actionable engineering plans while supporting team health and execution discipline.

### Responsibilities
- Manage delivery capacity, staffing, and engineering planning
- Support sprint and milestone planning with Product and Project Managers
- Help remove technical blockers and align cross-team dependencies
- Monitor team health, technical quality, and delivery risk
- Partner with Developers on design, estimation, and execution trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, high-quality engineering execution
- Improve team productivity and sustainable delivery pace
- Reduce delivery bottlenecks and technical debt risk

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and roadmap alignment
- Weekly delivery check-ins and dependency reviews
- Escalation discussions for staffing, quality, or delivery risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedule and dependency management
- Collaborates with Product Managers on scope trade-offs and delivery feasibility
- Supports Developers by clarifying priorities, technical planning, and decision-making

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure projects meet required security, privacy, and governance standards before release. They act as the guardrail for secure-by-design execution and policy alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Review project risks related to security, privacy, and compliance
- Define and validate security requirements for features and services
- Partner with engineering to assess controls, vulnerabilities, and mitigations
- Support release readiness and escalation for high-risk issues
- Contribute to incident response and post-incident improvements when needed

### Goals
- Reduce risk exposure and prevent preventable security issues
- Ensure compliance with required policies and stakeholder expectations
- Build trust through secure, well-governed delivery practices

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk assessments
- Release gating and compliance checkpoints
- Escalation paths for vulnerabilities, incidents, and policy exceptions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product and Project Managers to evaluate risk impacts and release timing
- Advises Developers on secure design, threat modeling, and remediation
- Coordinates with leadership and stakeholders when compliance or security decisions affect scope or schedule

---

## Customer Success / Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Adoption Leads connect delivery work to customer outcomes, usage patterns, and post-launch value realization. They help ensure the team remains oriented around customer value and adoption.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback, support issues, and adoption signals
- Translate customer needs into product and delivery priorities
- Partner with Product Managers on outcomes, messaging, and release readiness
- Help identify risks to adoption, retention, or user experience
- Support stakeholder communication around customer impact and value delivered

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption
- Ensure delivery work stays aligned to real user needs
- Surface customer insights earlier in the planning and execution cycle

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and adoption reports
- Cross-functional reviews with Product and delivery teams
- Stakeholder updates tied to customer outcomes and launch impact

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds customer insights to Product Managers for prioritization and roadmap tuning
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align launch timing and communication plans
- Provides context to Developers and PMs when usability or service issues affect delivery decisions

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define success metrics, monitor performance indicators, and help teams evaluate whether work is creating the expected value. They turn project activity into measurable outcomes and learning loops.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track key metrics for product, service, and operational outcomes
- Establish dashboards and reporting for project health and success measures
- Partner with Product and PM teams on measurement planning and decision-making
- Validate whether release changes improve adoption, quality, or business impact
- Surface statistical or operational risks that require follow-up

### Goals
- Make progress measurable and evidence-based
- Improve learning cycles and decision quality
- Connect delivery activity to customer and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Metrics reviews, dashboards, and stakeholder reporting
- Post-launch validation and outcome summaries
- Product and project review meetings focused on evidence and trends

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Product Managers with outcome tracking and prioritization evidence
- Helps Project Managers monitor project health, risks, and milestone readiness
- Works with Developers and QA to define measurable quality and reliability signals

---

## Delivery / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery and Release Managers coordinate the movement of work through release readiness, deployment, and operational handoff. They reduce execution risk by making sequencing, verification, and communication explicit.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, deployment windows, and activation sequencing
- Align engineering, QA, and stakeholder readiness before launch
- Track release dependencies, rollback readiness, and post-deployment checks
- Support incident communication and operational handoff when issues arise
- Maintain consistent release documentation and stakeholder updates

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and deployment disruption
- Improve operational readiness for each milestone or release
- Increase confidence in handoff from delivery to support and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment readiness reviews
- Pre-release coordination across teams and stakeholders
- Post-release verification and issue triage updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on milestone planning and risk review
- Coordinates with Engineering Managers and Developers to ensure release quality and readiness
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Customer Success leads to time launch communications and support planning

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help teams scale processes to real-world cross-functional delivery environments with clearer ownership and collaboration.
